🃏 MTG Card Scanner

Turn an Android phone and a tray into a hands-free card scanner. Artwork identification on the phone — no cloud AI, no API keys — with live Face to Face pricing and CSV / TXT export.

Couldn't read the latest release — open the releases page and download mtg-card-scanner-android.apk.

Then

  1. Open the APK on your Android phone (allow installs from your browser when Android asks).
  2. Open the app — the first time it downloads the card database (a few MB).
  3. Mount the phone over a tray, tap Area and drag a box around it. Drop cards in; they identify, price and file themselves on the phone.
  4. Optional: review and export on a computer — in the app, Settings → This phone's server → Pair a computer as admin, and open the link on the computer (same Wi-Fi or the phone's hotspot).

Your scans live only on the phone — uninstalling the app deletes them; export a CSV if you want a copy. Source & issues on GitHub.
